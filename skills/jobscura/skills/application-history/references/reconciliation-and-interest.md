# Reconcile history without flattening it

Treat these as different questions: did a message occur, who authored the specific
statement, whom did it concern, what was inferred then, and what applies now?
Keep exact source evidence separate from interpretation. Preserve ambiguity rather
than assigning another person's experience or job search to the user.

Multiple physical shares can be distinct events for one listing. Deduplicate
opportunity identity using reliable URL/requisition evidence without deleting the
meaningful repeated events. Do not count reposted copies as independent career
support or repeated shares as independent confirmed preferences.

## Attention, preference and motive

Attention means the user noticed or shared something. It might reflect curiosity,
a friend's search, timing, comparison or genuine personal interest. An explicit
motive statement is stronger evidence but still has a date and scope. Interest in
one role does not automatically establish a sector-wide preference or hard rule.
Keep alternative explanations, contrary statements and later corrections visible.

When a later source changes, retain the older event and its original interpretation
as history while reassessing current use. Do not silently rescore old opportunities,
revive a superseded preference or apply an expired condition to a new task.

## Corrections and recovered context

Check the whole nearby conversation when an isolated sentence seems inconsistent.
A correction to authority, factual ownership or career scale is not merely a tone
preference. Apply the corrected meaning to ongoing work and identify dependent
claims or drafts that may need review. Preserve the original exact version rather
than silently editing the source record.

A newly recovered source can change the completeness of a summary. State the
coverage inspected and what remains missing; no search result is proof of universal
absence. Where only a partial conversation or artifact is available, qualify the
conclusion and continue useful work that does not depend on the missing material.

When asked to continue a draft, select the actual latest chosen version and inspect
later edits. Do not overwrite a newer user change with an older assistant copy.
When asked what was submitted, seek submission evidence or a clearly identified
submitted artifact; a polished final draft alone is insufficient.

## Current job-state reconciliation

Use [application tracking](../../application-tracking/SKILL.md) for all-role audits,
retirement and repost checks. Preserve historical application/decision evidence
when a posting closes or a changed requisition appears. A new vacancy record must
link the old one rather than replace it; missing source access stays uncertain.
