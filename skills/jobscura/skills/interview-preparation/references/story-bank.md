# Private story bank

Keep the bank only in the user's selected private context, never in the public
skill package. Conversation-only preparation remains useful when nothing is saved;
claim persistence only after an authorized save succeeds.

For each real incident, record a theme, a short title, Situation, Task, Action,
Result and Reflection, plus "best for" question tags. Keep the user's contribution
and the outcome's limits visible; the shape does not justify filling unknowns.

Retain a provenance state for each story: from evidence (with its source),
user-stated on a date, or user-could-not-confirm. Withhold figures from a
user-could-not-confirm story. That state never becomes verified through age,
repetition or polished wording; new confirmation or evidence must be recorded
explicitly before changing it.

Apply the withdrawn-claims gate in
[evidence and currentness](../../application-writing/references/evidence-and-currentness.md)
before selecting or revising a story. A withdrawal in private `corrections[]`
blocks the claim in any wording until the user lifts it.

During a session, track which questions used each story. Notice repeated use across
questions and consider another eligible incident for breadth, without inventing
one or requiring variety at the expense of relevance.
