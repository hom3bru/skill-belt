# Assess fit without inventing experience

Compare the actual requirements to the relevant evidence. For each consequential
criterion state what is demonstrated, partially supported/transferable, unknown,
or contradicted, and why. Use source-linked examples at the right scale. Repeated
remit can matter more than a memorable event; a required specialist qualification
cannot be replaced by generic enthusiasm.

Preserve the boundary between support, advice, coordination, oversight and final
decision authority. Identify transferable work concretely: which function,
beneficiaries, constraints or decisions are similar, and which demands remain new?
Do not assume direct reports, budget ownership, technical implementation or formal
credentials from adjacent experience. Do not infer lack of experience solely from
missing accessible documentation.

A qualification-gap score is a judgment, not a fact or probability of hiring. If
the user's established scoring method is available, apply its meaning consistently
and explain the decisive evidence. Keep confidence separate. If the scale or
sufficient evidence is unavailable, leave the assessment unrated and explain what
would settle it rather than inventing a midpoint.

Interest is a separate dimension. A saved or repeatedly shared job indicates
attention, not necessarily motivation or preference. An explicit current statement
can clarify direction, but do not generalize a single job choice into a hard filter.
Pay, location, employment conditions and availability may be unknown even when the
qualification assessment is strong.

Useful delivery is compact: the recommendation, decisive matches/gaps, important
unknowns, verified source/currentness and a concrete next step. Include a full
criterion-by-criterion view only when it would help the user's decision. Do not
let an elaborate scoring sheet displace the actual career argument.

Preserve old judgments with their evidence and date. When the user requests a new
assessment after evidence changes, make the new basis explicit rather than silently
replacing the historical result. No score automatically authorizes a tracker edit,
application, fee, message or other external action; consult live permissions.
