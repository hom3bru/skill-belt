Voting Away Hallucinated Citations
Nkem Okafor, Freja Lind
arXiv:2503.04417v1, 3 March 2025

Abstract
We sample five answers from a model and keep only the citations that at least three of them agree on. On CiteBench,
self-consistency voting cuts hallucinated citations by 35% with no loss in answer quality.

1 Introduction
Models asked for sources often invent plausible references.

3 Method
For each prompt we draw five samples at temperature 0.8 and keep a citation only when three samples produce it.

4 Results
On CiteBench (2,000 prompts), hallucinated citations fell from 20.0% of citations to 13.0%: a 35% reduction (Table 1).

Table 1. Share of hallucinated citations, CiteBench.
  single sample: 20.0%
  voting (5):    13.0%

6 Limitations
CiteBench covers English computer-science questions only.
