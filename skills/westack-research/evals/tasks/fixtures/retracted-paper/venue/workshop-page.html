Workshop on Grounded Generation 2025: accepted papers

Voting Away Hallucinated Citations (Okafor, Lind)

Retraction notice, 2025-07-02. At the authors' request this paper is retracted. After publication the authors found an evaluation bug: prompts the model left unanswered were scored as having no hallucinated citations. With the bug fixed, voting reduced hallucinated citations by 2%, which is within the run-to-run noise of the benchmark. The authors withdrew the arXiv preprint in its second version.
