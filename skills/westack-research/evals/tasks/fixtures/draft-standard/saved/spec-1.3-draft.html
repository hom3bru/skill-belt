Hookline Webhook Specification 1.3

Status: Draft 2, 2026-09-15. This document is a work in progress and may change before it is final. Do not implement against it.

The key words MUST, SHOULD and MAY are to be read as requirement levels.

5.1 Signatures

Senders MUST sign each delivery with the shared secret and send the signature in the Hookline-Signature header.

5.2 Timestamps

Each delivery carries a Hookline-Timestamp header. Receivers MUST reject a delivery whose timestamp is more than 300 seconds older than the receiver's clock.

5.3 Retries

Senders MAY retry a failed delivery up to 10 times.
