Tenant Sharding at Scale
Samir Haddad, Claire Moreau
Proceedings of DBConf 2023, doi:10.5555/dbconf.2023.181

Abstract
We move a multi-tenant OLTP database from one shared cluster to one shard per tenant group and measure write latency
under a mixed read and write load.

5 Results
Under the mixed load, p99 write latency fell from 84 ms on the shared cluster to 41 ms with tenant sharding (Figure 4).
Median write latency barely moved (6 ms to 5 ms).

7 Limitations
One workload from one company; tenants were roughly equal in size.
