# Design: one-page checkout

## Goal

Cut checkout from three pages to one and lift completed orders.

## Proposal

- Shipping, payment and the order summary on one page, in that order.
- Guest checkout by default; an account is offered after the order is placed.
- The card form is the payment provider's hosted field, so card numbers never reach our servers.
- Address lookup by postcode for the UK and Germany at launch.

## Measures

Completed orders per checkout start, before and after, over four weeks.
