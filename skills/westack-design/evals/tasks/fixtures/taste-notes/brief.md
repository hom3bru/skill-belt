# Brief: Tally pricing page (tn-brief-1a5)

Tally sends invoices for freelancers. The pricing page lists the two plans side by side with their monthly price and what each includes.

- No plan is pushed: none is marked as recommended or styled to stand out, so people pick without pressure.
- Use the design record's colours, type and spacing; nothing outside it.
- Plain, legible, calm.
