## 1.0.0-alpha.63 (motion export), unreleased

westack-design 1.0.0-alpha.2. An approved design exported as a seamless loop (`references/motion-export.md`, linked from `motion.md`): filmed with the host's screencast tool for two or more cycles, its seam frames taken by pausing the animations at 0 and one period less a millisecond. `designcheck.py motion-export <folder>` reads the MP4's boxes with the standard library (32- and 64-bit sizes; stts frame counts; fragmented files' trun samples and their per-sample, fragment-default or track-default durations), decodes the two PNG frames (zlib and the five row filters) and refuses an unapproved design, a hash mismatch, a recording shorter than its cycles, a GIF without its repeat block, a missing reduced-motion answer and a seam where more than 0.5% of pixels differ. The first live screencast was fragmented with a near-empty moov, which the first reader took as a 0.065 s video; durations now come from the fragments too. Tests: 111 (13 new), with broken copies counting stts entries, ignoring 64-bit sizes, reading only the moov duration and skipping the seam. Fixture: `evals/fixtures/motion` (a pulse card on a 2 s cycle, approval.json). Ideas from gstack's design-motion at the mirror pin.
