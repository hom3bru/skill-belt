# Why I like cycling to work

Cycling to work give me time to think before the day starts. I used to take the bus, but it was always crowded and I arrived tired. Now I arrive awake, and my commute is faster then it was.

The biggest problem is the weather. When it rains I wear a jacket, and I still gets wet on the long hill near the station. Even so, I would not go back to the bus, because the ride is the best part of my day.
